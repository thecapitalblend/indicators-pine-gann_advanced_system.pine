# Gann Concepts — Source Reference

| Concept | Book / Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gann Angles (1x1, 2x1, etc.) | *Truth of the Stock Tape* (1923) | Price/time symmetry lines |
| Square of Nine | Hand-drawn spiral chart, referenced across his works | Cardinal (90°) / Ordinal (45°) cross levels |
| Time Cycles (45/90/120/144/180/270/360) | *45 Years in Wall Street* (1949) | Recurring "natural number" turn dates |
| Percentage Retracements | *Wall Street Stock Selector* (1930) | 1/8-step ratios, plus 1/3 and 2/3 |
| Rule of Thumb (count-based exhaustion) | *45 Years in Wall Street* | N consecutive same-direction closes signal exhaustion |
| Master Time Factor (60/82-year cycles) | Referenced in interviews/secondary sources, never fully published | Simplified bar-count analogue used here |
| Financial Astrology / Lunar Cycle | Hinted at in *The Tunnel Thru the Air* (1927) | Moon phase is real astronomy math; full planetary system was never fully documented by Gann himself |

## Honest scope note

Gann's complete, original trading system — including exactly how he combined
all of the above with his discretionary judgment — was never fully published,
and even his own students and historians disagree on the details. What's in
this repository are the concepts that are **publicly documented and
verifiable** from his books, implemented as literally as the math allows.

Any claim of a specific historical win-rate (e.g. "92% accuracy") should be
treated as unverified unless you can trace it to an audited, reproducible
source — this repo's `backtest.py` exists so you can generate your own
numbers on your own data instead of relying on secondhand claims.
